How the score works. Overall = 25% Audit Score + 75% Escalation Score. Audit Score = average audit score of all scored audits in the period (PDS, PDS–Mounjaro, Non Pharma, WLP–Non Pharma). Escalation Score = average of Consult, Pharmacy and Diagnostics escalation-free %, where escalation-free % = 100 × (1 − weighted tickets ÷ orders) and weighted tickets = 2 × High + 1 × Medium.
