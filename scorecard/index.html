How the score works. Every period starts at 100 and loses points for each problem found, heaviest first: 4 points per 1% of audits with a critical error (zero score), 3 points per High-severity ticket per 1,000 orders, 2 points per Medium-severity ticket per 1,000 orders, and 1 point per audit point missed on non-critical parameters. Ticket rates are the average of Consult, Pharmacy and Diagnostics, each against its own orders. RAG: green ≥ 85, amber 75–85, red < 75. Full method, High/Medium list and sources on the deep-dive page.
