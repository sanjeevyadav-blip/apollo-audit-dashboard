Critical-error definitions

Reference rubric behind the zero-score flags above — Pharma consults vs. DC 2.0 (video) clinics. Static reference — changes rarely.

Pharma consults

  • 1Doctor issues a prescription without calling the patient / eliciting details.
  • 2GP issues meds for ED, OCP, premature ejaculation, antipsychotics, immunosuppressants, anabolic/oral steroids, anti-cancer, anti-HIV, warfarin, disulfiram or other addiction/TB medication without a specialist screening.
  • 3Prescription written for stockpiling — especially antibiotics and other high-risk drugs.
  • 4Proxy doctor conducts the consultation.
  • 5Doctor shares a personal contact number with the patient.
  • 6Doctor issues an empty prescription.
  • 7Prescription issued with the wrong route of administration.
  • 8Prescription issued for multiple patients on a single script.
  • 9GP issues a prescription for injectables.
  • 10Prescription issued for veterinary use.
  • 11Prescription issued for veterinary use (duplicate entry in source).

Non‑Pharma · DC 2.0 clinics

  • 1Doctor issues a prescription without calling the patient / eliciting details.
  • 2Doctor doesn't consistently dress in professional attire on video, or the call background/audio is informal.
  • 3Doctor shares a personal mobile number, or collects the patient's.
  • 4Prescription issued on a personal clinic letterhead via photo, bypassing the platform.
  • 5Proxy protocol not followed.
  • 6Empty prescription sent, or notes irrelevant to the consult.
  • 7Proxy doctor conducts the consultation.
  • 8IM/IV injectables approved without proper guidance in general advice.
  • 9MTP kits prescribed over video consult.
  • 10Prescription issued with the wrong route of administration.
  • 11Consultation conducted off-platform — WhatsApp, Google Meet, Zoom, LinkedIn, etc.
